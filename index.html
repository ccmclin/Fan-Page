Meet Your Favorite Artist

Chappell Roan is an American singer and songwriter known for her theatrical performances, bold fashion, and catchy pop songs. Born Kayleigh Rose Amstutz in Missouri, she developed a distinctive musical identity that combines pop music with drag-inspired looks, camp, and storytelling.

"My name is Chappell Roan. I’m your favorite artist’s favorite artist. I’m your dream girl's dream girl and I’m gonna serve exactly what you are." (Coachella 2024)

Her debut album, The Rise and Fall of a Midwest Princess, was released in 2023. The album helped introduce a wider audience to her energetic sound and unapologetic approach to self-expression. Her music celebrates individuality, queer identity, and the freedom to be yourself.
